# WalkMe 👋

### The world is your museum.

WalkMe is a location-aware audio experience platform. We connect storytellers with explorers, turning every step into a narrative journey. By using precise geospatial technology, we trigger immersive stories exactly where they happened—allowing you to keep your eyes on the world, not your screen.

---

### 🚀 Status: Under Active Development

We are currently building the foundation of the WalkMe ecosystem. Our focus is on creating a seamless, "invisible" user experience for travelers and a powerful studio for creators.

*   **Phase 1**: Infrastructure & Stability (In Progress)
*   **Phase 2**: Creator Studio & Public Beta (Coming Soon)
